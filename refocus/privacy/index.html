Privacy Policy

Overview

Refocus ("the App") is developed by Indie Studio. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data Collection

Refocus does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your focus session data, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage frameworks. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
